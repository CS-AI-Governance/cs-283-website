## Attendance

Attendance at lectures is mandatory, except in cases of an OAE accommodation or
another valid reason brought to the course managers or your course assistant's
attention **at least 24 hours before** lecture. If you have a prolonged illness or
a personal situation that might lead to more than one absence, contact a member
of the course staff before missing a lecture.

Attendance is taken at the beginning of each class. Any student coming in late is
marked absent.

## How to prepare

In order to be prepared for in-class discussions, it is essential that you come to
each lecture having read and understood the assigned materials and having given
some thought to how the readings relate to the course in general. This will let
you benefit from the class presentations and discussions, and in turn prepare you
to discuss the issues in depth in section.

Come to class with considered views about:

- what the main claims offered in the texts or case studies are;
- the arguments offered in favor of these claims;
- whether these are good or plausible arguments;
- whether the claim is, all things considered, strong or plausible;
- what alternatives to the claims and arguments exist; and
- whether some alternative is superior to the claim under discussion.

Reading lists for each session are on the [lecture pages]({{ '/lectures/' | relative_url }}).
